FAQ

Common questions.

Straightforward answers about configuring and using WDJJ Timer.

How many rounds can I configure?

You can configure between 1 and 50 rounds.

Does the timer show milliseconds?

No. WDJJ Timer displays whole seconds so the clock remains easy to read across a gym.

What sounds play before a round?

The app plays a short beep for each step of the 3, 2, 1 countdown, followed by a longer signal when the round starts.

Why did my active timer reset?

The timer resets when WDJJ Timer moves into the background. This is intentional and ensures a match does not continue invisibly while another Apple TV app is open.

Does WDJJ Timer require an account?

No. The app has no account, sign-in, subscription, or advertising.

Does WDJJ Timer collect personal data?

No. WDJJ Timer does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. Read the complete Privacy Policy.

How do I request help or suggest a feature?

Email app-support@wallace.cloud with details about your question or idea.
