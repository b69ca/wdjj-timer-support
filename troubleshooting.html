Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for match day.

Work through the relevant steps below. Most issues are resolved by checking the Apple TV output or restarting the app.

IssuesNo timer soundRemote not respondingDisplay issueTimer unexpectedly resetContact support

No timer sound

  1. Raise the volume on the television, soundbar, or receiver.
  2. Confirm the Apple TV audio output is set to the speaker system you expect.
  3. Check that the television or receiver is not muted.
  4. Close WDJJ Timer, reopen it, and start a short test round.
  5. If other Apple TV apps are also silent, restart the Apple TV and check its audio settings.

The remote is not responding

  1. Press the Back button once, then try selecting the control again.
  2. Confirm the Siri Remote can navigate the Apple TV Home Screen.
  3. Recharge or reconnect the remote if it does not respond elsewhere.
  4. Force close WDJJ Timer and reopen it.

The timer does not fit or looks incorrect

  1. Check the television’s picture-size setting and disable zoom or overscan.
  2. Set the Apple TV video output to a resolution supported by the television.
  3. Close and reopen WDJJ Timer after changing display settings.

The timer unexpectedly reset

WDJJ Timer intentionally stops and resets the current match when the app goes into the background. Returning to the Apple TV Home Screen, opening another app, or restarting WDJJ Timer will return it to match setup.

Contact support

If the problem continues, email app-support@wallace.cloud. Please include:

  • Your Apple TV model and tvOS version
  • The WDJJ Timer version and build number
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • The steps that reproduce the issue
